# R-0019 — The x-ray finish and its sweep («Rayos X», «barrido»)

- **Status:** Accepted, In progress (implemented in the R-0017/R-0018 PR)
- **Milestone:** MC (the creator pipeline)
- **Owner:** Gustavo Delgadillo (westerngazoo)
- **Created:** 2026-11-22
- **Depends on:** R-0017 (surfaces, `Layer`, the finish trait, `Faceted`),
  R-0018 (meshes and the depth order that makes the batch)
- **Realized by:** [SPEC-0019](../specs/0019-x-ray.md)
- **QA:** `qa` agent run scoped to this requirement

## 1. Statement

motoreel must ship a second finish, **`XRay`** («Rayos X»), that shows a
figure «igual por fuera, distinto por dentro»: the skin becomes tinted
glass, the parts inside it show opaque inside a glow, and props keep the
faceted look. It must also ship **`Sweep`** («barrido»), which paints one
finish on one side of a moving image line and another on the other side,
so a reel can do the house's x-ray reveal.

## 2. Rationale

The x-ray sweep is the house's signature move («juguete + rayos X»,
rotorf-sico `docs/decisions.md`, 2026-11-18). Today it is drawn by hand in
2D (`juguete::rayos_x*`, and `barrido_x` copied 13 times in fbf). The 3D
spike (PR #8) showed it works on faceted meshes, with one defect the owner
saw: the glow is painted per face, so it **stacks** where neighbouring faces
meet. On 2026-11-22 the owner chose «facetas con rayos x» after the spike.

## 3. Acceptance criteria

- **AC1. Construction.** `XRay` is built from a `Faceted` (props and the
  inner parts' bands), a glass (tint, fill opacity, edge width and opacity)
  and a glow (width, opacity). `Sweep` is built from two finishes, a point
  on the line and the direction it moves. Bad input (an opacity outside
  `[0, 1]`, a negative or non-finite width, a non-finite point, a zero
  direction) is a typed error that names the field.
- **AC2. Props keep the faceted look.** A `Layer::Solid` surface is painted
  by `XRay` as exactly the `Prim2` `Faceted` paints for it, with the same
  band, so a prop looks the same on both sides of the sweep.
- **AC3. The skin is tinted glass, tinted once.** A run of consecutive
  `Layer::Skin` surfaces of one material is painted as **one** primitive:
  the union of their outlines, filled with the tint at the glass opacity
  and stroked with the facet edges. Over a flat background, no pixel
  inside two neighbouring skin faces is tinted twice.
- **AC4. The inside shows only under x-ray.** `Layer::Inner` surfaces are
  hidden by `Faceted` and shown by `XRay` opaque, in their material's tones,
  banded among the inner surfaces.
- **AC5. The glow does not accumulate.** An inner run's glow is one
  stroke-only primitive over the union of its outlines. Outside the core, no
  pixel is brighter than one layer of glow; a glow painted per face is
  shown, by the same check, to stack.
- **AC6. The sweep.** `Sweep` hands the **whole** batch to both finishes,
  so tones quantised over the figure do not change while the line moves,
  and clips each finish's output to its half-plane. A line past
  everything is exactly one finish. Where the line cuts an opaque face, the
  two halves meet with no background between them.
- **AC7. Determinism.** Two renders, and renders on four threads, are byte
  identical in both sinks. A trig-free golden per sink shows a skin cube
  with an inner octahedron and a prop, swept halfway. The finish code uses
  only `+ − × ÷`, `sqrt` and comparisons.
- **AC8. The kernel stays thin.** Zero new dependencies; in the core, not
  behind a feature (R-0017 AC9's test covers both).

## 4. Constraints & non-goals

- **A screen line, not a world plane.** The sweep is a half-plane in the
  image, which is what every house reel draws. A sweep across a world plane
  would need 3D clipping of the faces and is left for a later requirement.
- **Curved outlines are not split.** A subpath with a cubic piece goes whole
  to the side of its start point. Mesh faces are always straight.
- **Not bloom.** The glow is a flat halo of one opacity, composited once.
  Additive blending is a separate, later requirement (R-0016 §4).
- **Brand-agnostic.** Colours arrive as `Rgb`; the house cyan lives in the
  accounts.

## 5. Open questions

None. The tag question (R-0017 OQ-3) is answered there: the layer is an
attribute of the material.

## 6. Decision log

| Date | Decision | Rationale |
|------|----------|-----------|
| 2026-11-22 | **Accepted**, then **In progress**: the owner chose «facetas con rayos x» after the spike and said «dale con el 3D» | The owner's words, 2026-11-22 |
| 2026-11-22 | **One primitive per run of equal material** for glass and glow | A sink composites a primitive's fill and stroke once per pixel (nonzero union; stroke union by max), so neighbours cannot stack. The spike's per-face glow stacked |
| 2026-11-22 | **The sweep paints the whole batch twice and clips the outputs** | Clipping the batch instead would change the population `Faceted` quantises over, and tones would jump as the line moves. The halves are disjoint, so painting one after the other keeps depth order |
| 2026-11-22 | **Run, not material-wide, grouping** | Grouping all surfaces of a material at its first slot would hide a near part's glow under farther props drawn later. Two separate runs of one material that overlap on screen blend twice, as two lights would; within a run (a part, or neighbouring parts of one material) nothing stacks |
